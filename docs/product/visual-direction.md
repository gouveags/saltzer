# Visual and positioning direction

This direction was captured from Gabriel’s Saltzer landing-page concept on
2026-08-03. It is a tone and product-story reference, not a literal backlog or
screen specification.

## Preserve

- The lead promise: **“Focus on today. Everything else is noise.”**
- Quiet editorial typography, warm natural light, restrained color, generous
  space, precise borders, and a product that feels calm before it feels clever.
- A clear public story: Philosophy, Product, AI Context, CLI and API, Pricing,
  and About.
- The four proof pillars: Today is the product; opinionated like omakase; AI that
  understands context and behavior; private by design.
- Equal respect for web, mobile, CLI, and AI/agent surfaces.
- Future work surfaces on the intended day without controlling the present.
- Bootstrapping, profitability, real value, moral clarity, and healthy
  productivity are part of the company story rather than hidden founder notes.
- “Knowledge is knowing ahead of time” as the long-term intelligence ambition:
  the system should eventually solve problems before the person has to ask.

## Interpret, do not copy literally

The concept mock includes familiar task-manager navigation, six tasks in one
day, priorities, categories, inboxes, goals, and templates. Those elements make
the wider story legible, but importing them wholesale would contradict the
founding boundary and erase Saltzer’s advantage.

The product implementation therefore keeps:

- at most three outcome-level commitments;
- context beside intention;
- one active focus and concrete next move;
- explicit completion, release, and rescheduling;
- a fresh-day bias instead of an accumulating backlog;
- AI as a visible, permissioned collaborator.

Any future inbox, calendar, area, or goal surface must earn its place by making
today clearer. It may feed the Daily Contract; it may not turn Saltzer into a
configurable productivity operating system.

## Typography and interface voice

Saltzer uses Familjen Grotesk for display and reading text. Its visible ink
traps, sturdy uppercase forms, and high x-height make the interface feel like a
purpose-built field instrument instead of a generic editorial SaaS template.
Azeret Mono remains the operational voice for dates, state, capacity, and
actions. The contrast is deliberate: human decisions in Familjen, machine-
legible evidence in Azeret.

The product should feel like a calm field manual for the day: direct, warm,
precise, and a little unconventional. Avoid ornamental serif hero typography,
default browser focus treatments, feature-card marketing grids, and decoration
that is not rooted in the Convergent Day identity.

When Matthew 6:34 appears in product copy, use the verified NIV wording and
identify it as `MATTHEW 6:34 · NIV`; never present it as an unattributed maxim.

## Current product relationship

The authenticated application uses a darker, more focused editorial variant of
the same design language. The public site can use the concept’s brighter,
landscape-led presentation while the working surface stays low-distraction.
Shared typography, restraint, language, and product proof should make them feel
like one system rather than two visual brands.

## Brand mark

Saltzer's Convergent Day mark turns many incoming paths into one protected
center. One ember ray enters from below: the deliberate move that advances the
day. The mark replaces the placeholder circled `S` everywhere and must remain
legible as a favicon, native icon, compact product rail mark, and CLI-sized
glyph. Its default expression is ink and ember on warm paper; dark surfaces use
paper and a brighter ember without changing the geometry.
