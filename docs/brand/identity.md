# Saltzer identity

## Convergent Day

Saltzer's mark represents many demands resolving into one protected center.
The radial field is the world arriving; the octagonal salt grain is today; the
single ember ray is the deliberate move that advances it.

The identity is intentionally not a checkmark, checkbox, clock, brain, target,
or letter `S`. Those symbols describe task software in general. Convergent Day
describes Saltzer's opinion: capture can be broad, but attention must converge.

## System

- Ink: `#171A16`
- Warm paper: `#F1EBDD`
- Ember: `#E9653B` on light surfaces and `#F06A48` on dark surfaces
- Dark-surface mark: `#F0E9DA`
- Clear space: at least the width of the center grain on every side
- Minimum digital size: 24 px; prefer 40 px in product navigation
- Never rotate the mark, recolor individual rays, add effects, or place it in a
  decorative seal

The selected ImageGen source is retained as
`docs/brand/saltzer-convergent-day-source.png`. Production transparent marks
and platform icons live in `apps/product/assets/images/`. Run
`pnpm icons:generate` after changing the production light mark.
